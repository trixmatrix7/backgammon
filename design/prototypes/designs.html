Backgammon
art directions

Four complete directions, each with its own board, pieces, typeface, motion and sound. Every one has the table, lobby, scoreline and payout screens, and buttons to fire the dice, the callouts and the sound set so they can actually be judged rather than looked at. All four run on the same engine — the board's grid is measured out of the picture at runtime, so a new board drops straight in.

Neo Gammon

v1 · the first one · keep

Cel-shaded anime. Flat fills, thick ink outlines, one hard highlight, no gradients anywhere. Orange and cream on dusk lavender.

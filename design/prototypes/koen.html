Type 書体

Shippori Mincho for names and moments, Zen Maru Gothic for labels, JetBrains Mono for numbers. Serif carries the tone here, not weight.

Pieces 駒

Go stones. No outline — the form is one soft highlight and the shadow it casts. Shell white against slate.

Callout 演出

An ensō — a brushed ink circle with the stroke lifted at the top. Contained, quiet, gone in under half a second.

Sound 音

Warm and wooden. A stone set on a board, a soft rattle for the throw, a low hollow knock on a hit. Nothing sharp.

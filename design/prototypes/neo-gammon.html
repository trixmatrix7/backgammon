One art direction, built all the way out. The rule underneath it: cel shading is a construction method, not a filter — every shape is a flat fill, a thick ink outline, exactly one hard highlight and one flat shadow. No gradients, no blur, no glow anywhere on the screen. That is the whole difference between this and a dark UI with anime colours on it.

Type 書体

Zen Kaku Gothic New 900 for names and the action, skewed 7° so it reads as drawn rather than set. M PLUS 1 Code for every number. Bungee for the wordmark only.

Board 盤

Generated key art: orange and cream points on dusk lavender, ink outlines, screentone in the corners, hand-hatched centre bar. Everything on top is drawn in code so it can hold live states.

Pieces 駒

Flat fill, ink ring, one crescent highlight, and a coloured under-rim that gives the disc real thickness. Magenta rim = yours, cyan = theirs. Movable checkers physically lift.

Motion 動き

Nothing eases politely. Checkers land on an overshoot spring, the active plate is stamped at a tilt, and a hit fires a full impact frame — blowout, radial burst, katakana.

Sound 音

Short, dry, percussive. A woodblock knock for a placed checker, a rattle-and-clack for the throw, and a low hit with a metallic ring on top. Nothing sustained, nothing ambient.
